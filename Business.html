BUSINESS PLAN

1. BUSINESS IDEA

Mobile application for small businesses.

↓

2. PROBLEM

Small businesses need an easy way to manage customers, products and finances.

↓

3. SOLUTION

One app to manage customers, inventory, sales, income and losses.

↓

Customer management, inventory alerts, sales, income, losses and reports.

↓

5. TARGET CUSTOMERS

Small stores, local businesses and independent entrepreneurs.

↓

6. GROW THE BUSINESS

Improve the app, add new features and reach more businesses.
